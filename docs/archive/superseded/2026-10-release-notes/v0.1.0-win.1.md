## MTP3 cost per round (official NVFP4)

`--spec mtp --draft-tokens 3 --lm-head-draft`, non-thinking, natural window, INT8 KV,
`--prefill-chunk 128`, 128 output tokens, sampling temp 0.7 / top_p 0.8 / top_k 20 /
presence 1.5 / seed 42. ms/round = decode time ÷ MTP rounds.

| Context | Decode | MTP rounds | Decode time | ms/round | Accept length |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2,048 | 99.46 tok/s | 46 | 1.277 s | 27.76 | 2.76 |
| 8,192 | 85.50 tok/s | 54 | 1.485 s | 27.50 | 2.35 |
| 32,768 | 80.16 tok/s | 53 | 1.584 s | 29.89 | 2.40 |
| 65,536 | 74.15 tok/s | 51 | 1.713 s | 33.59 | 2.49 |
| 131,072 | 55.32 tok/s | 57 | 2.296 s | 40.28 | 2.23 |
| 204,800 | 49.01 tok/s | 54 | 2.591 s | 47.98 | 2.35 |

The per-round cost rises slowly with occupied context while the acceptance length stays around
2.2–2.8 tokens; the decode-rate drop from 2K to 200K is the per-round cost, not a loss of
acceptance. Source: `_scratch/bench-bc-dflash2-20261018/result-Nm-{2k,8k,32k,64k,128k,200k}.json`.

## 196K window invariance

The same 2K/8K/32K prompts were re-run with the context window forced to 196,608
(`--max-context 196608 --kv-capacity 196608`) instead of the natural prompt+1K window:

| Run | Natural window | Decode | 196,608 window | Decode | Diff |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| control 2K | 3,072 | 40.45 tok/s | 196,608 | 40.51 tok/s | +0.15% |
| MTP3 2K | 3,072 | 99.46 tok/s | 196,608 | 101.62 tok/s | +2.17% |
| control 8K | 9,216 | 38.18 tok/s | 196,608 | 38.15 tok/s | −0.08% |
| MTP3 8K | 9,216 | 85.50 tok/s | 196,608 | 85.37 tok/s | −0.15% |
| control 32K | 33,920 | 34.76 tok/s | 196,608 | 34.58 tok/s | −0.52% |
| MTP3 32K | 33,920 | 80.16 tok/s | 196,608 | 79.98 tok/s | −0.22% |

Decode speed is unchanged within run-to-run noise (−0.5%…+2.2%): the configured window does not
enter the decode cost on this workload. Source: `_scratch/bench-bc-dflash2-20261018/win196608/`.

## B/C text-only parity

B (`Qwen3.8-27B-Coder390-W4A4-MTP-DFlash2-NInferV2-NOT-FOR-V3.ninfer`, V2 text-only layout) and
C (`Qwen3.8-27B-Coder390-EfficientThink-W4A4-MTP-DFlash2.ninfer`, V3 layout) carry the same
Coder390 W4A4 weights. With identical prompts and settings:

- **Greedy:** the 128 generated token IDs are bit-identical at 2K and 8K context.
- **Sampling (non-thinking):** decode rates agree to under 0.4% — 40.54 vs 40.46 tok/s (2K),
  38.16 vs 38.13 (8K), 34.72 vs 34.72 (32K, both stopped at 121 tokens), 29.08 vs 28.97 (64K).

Conclusion: the text-only V2 artifact produces the same text as the V3 artifact, so the V2
path in this release is a load-format difference, not a model difference.
Source: `_scratch/bench-bc-dflash2-20261018/result-{B,C}-{2k,8k}-greedy.json` and
`summary.json`.

## Limitations

- **Text-only; media stubbed.** `NINFER_STUB_MEDIA=ON` omits FFmpeg/libcurl: image/video prompts
  and HTTP(S) media acquisition raise a clear runtime error. See
  [docs/windows.md](../../../windows.md#media-is-stubbed-in-this-build).
- **`SystemMonitor` is empty.** Its host/GPU sampling is `/proc`-based on Linux and returns no
  rows on Windows; the monitor page has no data. Serving is unaffected.
- **No independent cross-platform numeric baseline.** These binaries have not been numerically
  cross-checked against a Linux build on the same artifacts; the gates above (reader, graph
  parity, serving smoke) verify behavior, not cross-platform bit-exactness.
